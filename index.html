Bearded Iris

Bearded Iris

Blooms:
Spring, April-May


Wilts:
?


Seeds Available:
?


Planting Seeds:
Autumn, October
3 inches apart, 3/4 inch deep


Propagating by Division:
Late Summer, July-August
Trim foliage down to approx. 6 inches


Planting Rhizomes:
6-12 inches deep, cover roots but let the top of the rhizome show above soil
Use low nitrogen fertilizer to give roots nutritional boost and help them recover from the transplant process
Water, newly set plants need moisture to help their root systems become established
